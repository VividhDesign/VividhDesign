# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-21)

First release.

* Citation grammar for SCC, SCR, AIR, SCC OnLine, INSC and High Court neutral citations, SCALE, JT,
  Cri LJ, MANU, ITR, Taxmann and LiveLaw, with parallel citations, case names, pinpoints and quotes.
* Verification against the official Supreme Court Reports (38,413 judgments, 1950–2026).
* Open citation concordance mined from Supreme Court and High Court judgments, with a guard against
  judgments that quote fabricated citations.
* Quote verification against judgment text.
* Command line (`check`, `sync`, `mine`, `serve`), web interface, and an in-browser build that keeps
  documents on the user's machine.
* Held-out evaluation (`eval/evaluate.py`).
