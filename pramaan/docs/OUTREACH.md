# Outreach tracker

Copy-ready messages are in [LAUNCH.md](LAUNCH.md). Check every address on the organisation's own site
before sending; some below come from third-party listings.

**Write in your own words.** LiveLaw states that it rejects article submissions containing AI-generated
or AI-rewritten text. Editors elsewhere can tell too. Use the drafts in LAUNCH.md as notes, then write
the actual email or article yourself. It reads better coming from you, and it is your name on it.
