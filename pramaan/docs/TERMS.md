# Terms of use (hosted Pramaan service)

*A plain-language starting point. Have a lawyer review it before you charge customers.*

1. **What the service does.** Pramaan compares case citations in a document with open records of
   Indian judgments and reports what it finds. It does not give legal advice.
2. **No guarantee.** Findings are evidence to check, not rulings. Open records are incomplete: a genuine
   judgment can be reported as "not found", and a mistaken citation can go undetected. You remain
   responsible for verifying every authority you rely on, as the Supreme Court required in
   *Pooja Ramesh Singh v. J&K Bank* (2026 INSC 668).
3. **Do not use a finding alone to accuse anyone** of citing fabricated authority. Read the judgment
   first.
4. **Acceptable use.** Do not attempt to overload, reverse-engineer the key system of, or resell access
   to the hosted service without agreement. Fair-use limits apply to free access.
5. **Fees.** Paid plans are billed monthly in advance, based on the plan or the usage log. Unused
   requests do not carry over. Either side may cancel at the end of a billing month.
6. **Liability.** To the extent the law allows, liability is limited to the fees paid in the three
   months before a claim. There is no liability for decisions taken on the basis of a finding.
7. **Data.** See the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md). Documents are processed in memory and not stored.
8. **Open source.** The software is MIT-licensed, and you may run it yourself. These terms cover only
   the hosted service.
9. **Data attribution.** Judgment data: Indian Supreme Court Judgments and Indian High Court Judgments
   (Dattam Labs, AWS Open Data Registry), CC-BY-4.0.
10. **Governing law.** India; courts at [your city].
