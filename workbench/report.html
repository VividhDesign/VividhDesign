Strata: 8-bit quantization (built today)

Your README listed "no vector compression" as the top limitation. Strata now has quantization="sq8", with three modes:

  • SQ8 + rerank: the graph stores 1 byte per dimension, and the top candidates are re-ranked with exact float distances. Recall matches the float index (0.9335 vs 0.9334 on GloVe-100 at ef=128).
  • SQ8 + rerank + Index.load(path, mmap_vectors=True): the float vectors stay on disk and only the graph and codes use RAM.
  • SQ8 without rerank: the index is half the size (102 vs 209 MiB on GloVe-100) for about 1.5 points less recall, with +20% all-core throughput.

The kernels are AVX2, NEON and scalar. NEON was verified on x86 through an emulation header, and your existing macOS CI will check it on real ARM.
